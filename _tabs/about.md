I'm interested in understanding how cyber attacks happen, what they leave behind, and how to investigate them.

My main focus is Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR), particularly network forensics, endpoint investigations, malware analysis, and threat detection.

I've spent time working through cybersecurity labs and hands-on challenges, using platforms such as Hack The Box to develop my technical foundations. I'm now focusing on building practical investigation skills and learning how to approach security incidents from initial detection through to understanding what actually happened.

## Areas of Interest

- Network Forensics
- Digital Forensics and Incident Response
- Malware Analysis
- Threat Hunting
- Security Operations

## What You'll Find Here

This blog is where I document investigations, lab exercises, technical research, and things I learn along the way.

I'll focus on the process behind each investigation: the evidence, the tools, the analysis, and the conclusions. I want each write-up to explain not just what I found, but how I got there.

The goal is simple: keep learning, improve my analytical skills, and build a solid body of practical work in cybersecurity.
